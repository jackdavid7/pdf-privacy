Privacy Policy

PDF Privacy for Mac

PDF Privacy collects no data. Your files, and everything in them, stay on your Mac.

Your files

PDF Privacy opens, changes and saves files on your Mac only. It never uploads a file, a page, a word or a picture from a file to anyone, us included. It works the same with no internet connection.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-in and no account. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code that reports anything. We do not know who uses it or how.

What stays on your Mac

  • Earlier versions of a file you save, so you can go back with File, Revert To. They are kept in the app's own folder on your Mac and you can remove them at any time.
  • Your settings, recent files and saved signatures, in the same folder.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Camera and Contacts

The app asks for the camera only when you take a signature with it, and uses it only while you do. Nothing is recorded or kept except the signature you choose to save.

It asks for Contacts only when you pick AutoFill, and reads only your own card, to put your name and address into a form. Nothing from Contacts leaves your Mac.

Signing on your iPhone

If you choose to sign on your iPhone, your Mac shows a code to scan and serves one signing page on your own Wi-Fi network for that single signature. The signature goes straight from the iPhone to the Mac. It never passes through the internet or any server of ours. The Mac stops listening as soon as the signature arrives, when you close the window, or after 10 minutes.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
